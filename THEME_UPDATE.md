## Design Psychology:

### Blue = Trust
- Used by educational institutions globally
- Associated with intelligence, confidence, reliability
- Perfect for UPSC (serious government exam prep)

### Cyan/Teal = Professional
- Modern but not trendy
- Clean, technical, precise
- Common in professional tools

### Maintained:
- ✅ Green for success/good scores (encouragement)
- ✅ Orange for warnings/average (motivation)
- ✅ Red for poor scores (alert)
- ✅ Glassmorphism effect (modern feel)
